## Key Value Propositions by Category

### For law firms:
"See the full compliance picture before taking on an employer defense case or conducting M&A due diligence. Our database shows whether an employer with an active NLRB charge also has OSHA violations or wage theft findings — information that's currently scattered across three separate federal systems."

### For ESG/data companies:
"Quantitative labor governance signals from actual federal enforcement actions — not self-reported ESG disclosures. NLRB unfair labor practice charges, OSHA fatality records, and WHD back wages ordered, all linked to the same employer via name normalization and fuzzy matching."

### For researchers:
"A novel dataset connecting labor relations (NLRB), workplace safety (OSHA), and wage compliance (WHD) at the employer level. Enables cross-agency research questions that have been impractical due to data silos."

### For journalists:
"Structured, searchable data showing which employers face repeated federal labor enforcement actions across multiple agencies. Query by employer, state, date range, case type. Every record quality-scored, all extraction rule-based and reproducible."

## Timing Notes
- Starbucks unionization wave (2022–present) makes NLRB data especially timely
- Amazon labor organizing across multiple facilities creates interest in multi-employer analysis
- DOL "Project Firewall" initiative (cross-agency enforcement coordination) aligns with this project's approach
- Growing ESG investor interest in labor practices creates demand for quantitative data
