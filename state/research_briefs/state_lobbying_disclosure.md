# State Lobbying Disclosure Aggregator

## Research Date: 2026-03-02

## Concept
Aggregate state-level lobbying registration and expenditure data from all 50 state disclosure systems into a unified, searchable database. Cross-link lobbyists and clients across states to show multi-state influence campaigns.

## Data Gap Assessment: 2/5
OpenSecrets already aggregates state lobbying data for 20 states (the states that provide meaningful electronic disclosure). FollowTheMoney.org (National Institute on Money in State Politics) has been working toward a 50-state lobbying expenditure database. Only 19 of 50 states make meaningful expenditure data available electronically. Many states use paper forms or have minimal electronic disclosure. This space already has well-funded nonprofit competitors working on the same problem.

Score: 2/5 — OpenSecrets and FollowTheMoney already serve this market. We'd be duplicating their multi-year, well-funded efforts.

## Technical Feasibility: 2/5
- 50 different state disclosure systems
- Many states use paper forms or proprietary web apps
- No standard data schema across states
- OpenSecrets has a team dedicated to this and still only covers 20 states
- Montana stopped electronic disclosure in 2019, reverting to paper

## Rejection Reasoning
OpenSecrets (well-funded nonprofit) and FollowTheMoney are direct competitors with years of head start. Technical feasibility is low due to the fragmented state disclosure landscape. Even with significant effort, we'd produce an inferior product to organizations with dedicated teams working on this specific problem.
